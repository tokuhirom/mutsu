# say/put through a user print, Junction.THREAD and Cool.encode are rows

Three more by-name arms of the method cascade are now late rows of the method table, each owned by the type Rakudo
declares it on (ADR-11276 §9.142):

- `say`/`put` with arguments on a class that defines its own `print` (`Mu`);
- `THREAD` on a junction (`Junction`);
- `encode` for every receiver the `Str` rows do not take (`Cool`).

The answers are unchanged.
