RakuAST now preserves the source of nonplain `qw`, `q:w` and `q:ww` quotes. It renders quoted words and interpolating segments with Rakudo's `QuotedString` and `QuoteWordsAtom` shapes, skips embedded comments, and keeps `Qww` text literal. An unknown `:b` backslash escape reports its typed parse error across the RakuAST boundary. The quoting and Advent 2014 roast files now pass in RakuAST frontend mode.
