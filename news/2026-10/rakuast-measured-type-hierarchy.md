# RakuAST type hierarchy measured from rakudo

The RakuAST type objects and nodes now answer `.^mro`, `.^parents`, `.^roles`,
`.^isa`, `.isa`, `.does`, `.HOW` and smartmatch from a table measured from
rakudo 2026.09 (`src/rakuast/hierarchy.tsv`, regenerated by
`scripts/rakuast-hierarchy.raku`). The hand-kept "semantic ancestor" lists and
the rule that made a class a subtype of its `::` namespace are gone.

Before, 431 of the 511 names in rakudo's `RakuAST::` tree had a different MRO:
`RakuAST::Class`, `RakuAST::ColonPair` and every `Circumfix` were no
`RakuAST::Term`, no literal was a `RakuAST::Literal` or `RakuAST::Termish`,
`RakuAST::Statement::If` missed its `IfWith` parent, and no node did any of
the RakuAST roles (`RakuAST::Sinkable`, `RakuAST::Code`, ...). A walker that
dispatches on those types — `RakuAST::Deparse`'s multis, Code::Coverable's
`when RakuAST::Statement` — saw different nodes than on rakudo. Every class's
MRO and every class's and role's roles now match rakudo's.

The bare `RakuAST` namespace and an indirect `::('RakuAST::IntLiteral')`
lookup resolve as well.
