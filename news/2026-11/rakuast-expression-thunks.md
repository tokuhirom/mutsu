RakuAST expressions now expose their parsed WhateverCode prime thunks and lazy list-infix block thunks through `visit-thunks`. The thunk objects answer `thunk-kind` and `thunk-details`, and `Node.dump` prints their `🧠` lines before expression children. The existing curry boundary and compile-time-value model determine where those thunks belong, so `EVAL($ast)` keeps the same behavior.
