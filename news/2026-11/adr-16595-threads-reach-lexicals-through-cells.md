# ADR-16595: threads reach lexicals through their captured cells

ADR-16595 supersedes ADR-12204. Code on another thread now reaches a user lexical through the cell its closure captured, and no user lexical is published into the name-keyed `shared_vars` store, which is rakudo's model. A probe over all 456 `t/concurrency/**` files motivated the change. Giving a declaration-site cell to every container an escaping closure captures, whether or not ADR-0055 vouches for it, is green. The collisions that remain once seeding is filtered come from operations that read and write the lane by name (`cas` by name, and a worker's `push` routed into `__mutsu_atomic_arr::`). The ADR replaces ADR-12204's remaining slices with five: declaration cells, hyper/race bodies as captures, place-addressed atomics, no seeding plus deleting the masks, and the opaque remainder through ADR-12529's static link.
